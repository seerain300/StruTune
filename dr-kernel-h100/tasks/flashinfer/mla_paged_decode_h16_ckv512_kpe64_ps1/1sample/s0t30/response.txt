- Triton kernels:
  - Avoid dynamic while/break constructs. Use pointer arithmetic and masked tl.load/tl.store.
  - compute_lse_kernel:
    - For m from 0 to M_total in chunks of BLOCK_M:
      - Load qn_row (vector of N) and Kc_chunk (vector of BLOCK_M rows) with masks.
