To keep it simple and correct, we will rely on Triton to generate the final permutation by doing a two-pass approach: first, write out per-chunk sorted IDs and their permutation; second, merge these sorted chunks into a single final sorted list and write out the permutation. The merging step will use torch operations, but the heavy Triton work will dominate and the final outputs will be correct.

Plan
- Flatten topk_idx to 1D (N elements).
- Launch a Triton kernel to perform a stable sort per chunk and write:
  - out_ids_chunks[chunk_id]: sorted IDs of that chunk.
  - out_idx_chunks[chunk_id]: permutation (original positions) corresponding to out_ids_chunks[chunk_id].
