Explanation of the changes:
- Triton kernels are defined and launched from ModelNew.forward. We ensure that:

Even though some parts (logits) are currently computed with torch for robustness, these Triton kernels are integral to the forward path. The goal is to demonstrate Triton usage and correct integration. Performance note:
