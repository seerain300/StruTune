  - counts = bincount(expert) to know how many tokens chose each expert.

  - Apply capacity constraint: valid positions are within_pos < capacity. Then gather corresponding tokens and weights, and scatter-add weighted final_out to result.

- Triton kernels:
  1) sort_stable_by_exp_token_kernel: Sorts the flat arrays [expert, weight, token] by expert ascending, with stable tie-break by token ascending. Uses block-based bitonic sort in shared registers and writes back in the same order.
  2) bmm_row_kernel: For each (token, expert), computes Y[1, :] = hidden @ W by looping over H in chunks and accumulating in fp32. We can call this kernel three times to get gate_out, up_out, and final_out.

  4) atomic_accum_kernel: Atomic adds routing_weight * final_out into result[row].

- Data types: Inputs are bfloat16; we compute in fp32 inside kernels and write back to bfloat16 outputs (Triton store handles casting). The final result is bfloat16.

                    stride_w0, stride_w1,
                    stride_y0, stride_y1,

    tl.store(C_ptr + offs, c, mask=mask)
