  5) Output gating: y = C * conv_out.
  6) Final output projection: y_T = y.transpose(-1, -2) -> (B, S, H), then output = F.linear(y_T, out_proj_weight, out_proj_bias), with out_proj_weight (H, H), out_proj_bias (H,). Final output is (B, S, H).
