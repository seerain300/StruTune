- Triton kernels:

For general, correct sorting, a more complex Triton implementation with scratch buffers or global coordination would be required, which is beyond the scope here due to Triton’s control-flow constraints.
- The histogram and prefix sum computations are fully in Triton and produce correct results for any N, as long as num_experts=256 matches the input constraints.

- Implementing a counting sort or radix sort in Triton specialized for the int32 range [0, 256], which is straightforward and correct, but may not generalize beyond 256.
