Notes on correctness and performance

- Correctness: The Triton kernels handle all math. We build L from A_cumsum via cumsum and exp with lower-triangular masking. We compute G by contracting B and C after expanding groups to heads. We then directly compute Y_diag by looping over j and d inside the Triton contraction kernel, multiplying by L on the fly (we assume L is available as a tensor computed by build_L_kernel). This avoids forming the intermediate M tensor and adheres to Triton-only computation, given the 3D grid constraint.
- Performance: The approach avoids multiple torch elementwise kernels by fusing contraction and summation into Triton. The cumsum for L is computed per (b, h, i) in a Triton kernel. While we use loops over CHUNK_SIZE and HEAD_DIM (both constants), this still reduces host overhead and leverages Triton’s elementwise and reduction capabilities.
- Limitations: Triton does not support 5D grids directly. We folded dims into 3D by using program_id packing and inner loops. This ensures that all math happens in Triton and no torch operations are used in forward beyond tensor allocations and dtype casts at the end.
