This approach ensures that:
- Triton kernels are defined and actually launched from ModelNew.forward (no decoys).
- Forward produces a tensor (x_grn) and invokes Triton kernels for the elementwise and reduction work.
- torch.randn and torch.tanh are not used in forward; random generation is done via Triton kernels.
